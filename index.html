FAQ

Questions before deployment.

What does Unburden measure?

Unburden is designed to produce species-specific, CFU-equivalent measurements that reflect viable organisms relevant to production decisions.

Who operates the system?

The workflow is being designed for easy use by QC line operators, without requiring specialist molecular microbiology skills.

How is the method validated?

Each pilot runs alongside the facility’s existing testing method so performance and usability can be evaluated in the relevant commercial context.

Where do pilots run?

At customer facilities. Independent operation in the environment where the system will be used is central to the pilot.
